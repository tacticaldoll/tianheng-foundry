# Routing Dispositions

This skill retires prose; it performs no conversion, amendment, baseline operation, or upstream
work. The retained list is its second output and routes into workflows that already exist.

## Inherited Disposition Table

The judgement of what may leave the tree is `forge-law`'s, not this skill's. Its
`authority-transition.md` Prose Disposition table governs here unchanged:

| Prose content | Disposition |
|---|---|
| Fully duplicated live structural claim | Removal only under an explicit replacement diff |
| Structural claim broader than observation | Narrow prose to the ungoverned remainder |
| Historical rationale | Keep in project decisions or commit provenance |
| Process or approval rule | Keep in contributor/agent governance |
| Judgment guidance | Keep for human review |
| API explanation | Keep in product docs or rustdoc |
| Generated projection body | Never hand-edit; regenerate from Constitution |

Two rows are read more narrowly here than in `forge-law`, because this skill sweeps prose nobody is
converting:

- **Fully duplicated live structural claim.** `forge-law` has just proven a reaction for the very
  sentence in front of it. This skill has not proven anything; it has only matched an existing
  boundary. So removal additionally requires the enforced and unbaselined conditions in
  `coverage-criterion.md`.
- **Structural claim broader than observation.** `forge-law` may narrow the prose to the ungoverned
  remainder as part of a proven transaction. This skill may not: narrowing a sentence is a prose
  replacement with no proof behind it. The claim is retained whole and routed.

## Routes

| Finding | Route | What the receiving skill is being asked |
|---|---|---|
| `WIDER` | `amend-law` | Can the existing boundary be tightened, or its target retargeted, to observe the surplus? |
| `TOOTHLESS` | `amend-law` | Should this boundary's severity be raised so the claim is actually held? |
| `ABSORBED` | `manage-baseline` | Is this entry still real debt, and who owns closing it? |
| `UNGOVERNED` | `forge-law` | Is this claim admissible as a new boundary candidate? |
| `UNOBSERVABLE` | `shape-capability` | Is there a real observation source for a bounded upstream candidate? |

A route is a question, not a decision. Do not pre-answer it, do not draft the amendment or
candidate, and do not imply that the receiving skill will accept. Each route carries its own
authority gate, and none of them is opened by a retirement sweep.

## Nothing To Route

An empty retained list is a normal outcome and worth stating, because it means every structural
claim in the swept subtree is either held with teeth or absent. An empty **retired** list is also
normal: `NOTHING_COVERED` says the sweep ran and found no redundancy, which is a different report
from a sweep that could not run.
