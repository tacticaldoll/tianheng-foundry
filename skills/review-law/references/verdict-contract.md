# Verdict Contract

The verdict describes candidate readiness, not policy acceptance.

## `REJECT`

Use when revision requires renewed authority or a different fundamental design:

- no valid intent authority;
- silent or unauthorized amendment/baseline change;
- unobservable claim or reason overreach central to the candidate;
- deliberate reaction evasion;
- wrong source of law; or
- capability absent from supported Tianheng.

## `REVISE`

Use when the intended candidate may remain but evidence or execution is incomplete:

- missing violating or precision witness;
- invalid exit capture or exit `2`;
- warn/baseline substituted for enforcement proof;
- redundant reaction that can be removed without changing intent;
- stale or hand-inconsistent projection;
- incomplete prose disposition;
- unsupported/unknown compatibility evidence pending investigation; or
- unrelated changes that should be split.

## `ACCEPT_CANDIDATE`

Use only when:

- every gate passes with cited evidence;
- no blocking finding remains;
- required before/after and precision directions are proven;
- projection and compatibility are fresh; and
- residual risks are explicitly named.

The wording must remain `ACCEPT_CANDIDATE`. Never shorten it to `ACCEPTED`, `APPROVED`, or
`AcceptedLaw`; a human/steward still owns that transition.

## Finding Severity

Use `REJECT` or `REVISE` on each blocking finding to show its required disposition. Non-blocking
notes belong under residual risk, not mixed into the blocking list.
