# Reaction Evidence Audit

## Formation

Require:

- a violating witness at the declared target/depth with enforced exit `1`;
- a nearby legitimate precision witness with exit `0`;
- structured target/rule/fact evidence tying the result to the candidate; and
- repository-native gates after focused proof.

Warn-only, fully baselined, exit `2`, successful construction, compilation, and projection do not
prove teeth.

## Amendment

| Class | Required changed witness | Required stable witness |
|---|---|---|
| Tighten | Clean before, enforced after | Adjacent allowed fact stays clean |
| Loosen | Enforced before, clean after | Adjacent forbidden fact stays enforced |
| Retarget | New target reacts after | Old target matches declared disposition |
| Reason | Reaction identity/outcome stable | Projection changes only within bounded reason |
| Remove | Enforced before, clean after | Unrelated accepted boundary still reacts |

## Evidence Integrity

Check exact commands, process status, fixture source, manifest scope, runner identity, and baseline
mode. A formatter pipeline may swallow Tianheng's exit status. A demo constitution may not be the
adopter's law. A fixture using a second hand-built constitution may not test the candidate source.

When structured identity exists, compare `target`, `rule_key`, and `fact`; diagnostic wording alone
is not stable identity.

## Missing Evidence

Report the smallest exact proof needed. Do not add fixtures or rerun with weakened flags as part of
review.
