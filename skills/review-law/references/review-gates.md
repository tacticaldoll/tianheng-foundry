# Sequential Review Gates

Run gates in order because later proof cannot repair an invalid premise.

| Gate | Pass Evidence | Blocking Failure |
|---|---|---|
| Authority | Exact intent source; explicit amendment/baseline authority where needed | Invented policy, silent amendment, unauthorized baseline |
| Observability | Every reason clause maps to target, observation, rule, and depth | Unobservable or overstated claim |
| Reaction | Required changed and stable witnesses produce structured expected outcomes | Missing, invalid, inert, or imprecise proof |
| Minimality | One necessary reaction per fact; no dilution or unrelated policy | Redundancy, weak severity, hidden scope |
| Projection / prose | Fresh generated context; honest source-prose disposition | Hand edit, stale projection, deleted ungoverned prose |
| Compatibility | Supported Tianheng line and verified public vocabulary | Unknown or unsupported surface |

## Gate Dependency

- Failed authority invalidates the candidate regardless of technical quality.
- Failed observability means more tests cannot make the stated claim true.
- Failed reaction evidence may be repairable without redesign.
- Failed minimality may reveal a policy-shape problem even when tests pass.
- Fresh projection reflects source; it does not prove source correctness.

Continue after a blocking failure only to identify independent findings, not to average gates into a
passing score.
