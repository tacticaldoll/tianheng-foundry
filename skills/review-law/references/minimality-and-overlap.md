# Minimality And Overlap

More reactions do not necessarily mean stronger governance.

## Redundancy

Flag:

- a deny rule for a fact already excluded by a closed allowlist;
- duplicate boundaries observing the same target, fact, severity, and perimeter;
- a new boundary whose target is unreachable or whose evaluator is not composed;
- copied law sources or test-only constitutions that can drift from accepted source; and
- prose requirements restated in reasons beyond what the reaction observes.

Redundancy increases amendment and context surface while adding no independent reaction.

## Dilution

Treat these as policy effects, not harmless implementation choices:

- `.warn()` replacing enforcement;
- a new baseline entry;
- wider allowlist;
- shallower scan;
- narrower target;
- removed semantic/runtime option;
- excluded workspace member; or
- reason wording that normalizes a previously forbidden fact.

Require explicit amendment/baseline authority and directional proof.

## Scope Integrity

One candidate should carry one cohesive policy transaction. Flag unrelated boundary edits,
opportunistic cleanup, broad cookbook adoption, and new abstraction layers without a second use.

Minimality does not mean the fewest lines. It means the smallest complete reaction and proof set
that protects the declared reason.
